# Interview Preparation Strategy

When answering SQL interview questions:

1.  Understand the business problem.
2.  Identify required tables.
3.  Determine relationships.
4.  Decide between JOIN, CTE, or Subquery.
5.  Think about performance.
6.  Validate results.

# Data Engineer Insight

A successful Data Engineer writes SQL that is **correct, readable,
scalable, and performant**. Beyond syntax, employers value your ability
to understand business requirements, model data, optimize queries, and
produce trusted datasets for analytics platforms.
